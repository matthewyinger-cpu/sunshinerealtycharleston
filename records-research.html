How I came to this

A name beside the land

I came to family history sideways. While tracing the title to a tract along French Quarter Creek in Berkeley County, I kept seeing the name Russ beside the land in the St. Thomas and St. Denis Parish records. It caught my eye because the name ran in my own family. I followed it back, and it was no coincidence: John Russ was my direct ancestor. That line is now documented fourteen generations, back to England around 1571.

That project taught me what this work takes: patience with old handwriting, knowing which courthouse room holds which book, and never accepting a link that isn't backed by a record in hand. In every case I keep two columns: what the documents prove, and what is still a working theory.
